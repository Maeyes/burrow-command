# BunnyWorldHero reference analysis

## Identification

- Subject: stylized anthropomorphic rabbit character turnaround; `primaryDomain: character`; confidence 0.99.
- Reconstruction target: the neutral standing design shown in front, three-quarter, side, and rear views. Expression and action panels are secondary evidence for facial ranges and future articulation only.

## Overall form and silhouette

- Bilaterally symmetric, organic character with a 1.0 m total height including ears.
- Primary silhouette is a wide rounded head over a compact pear/egg torso, with two extremely long laterally hanging ears. Limbs are short and terminate in rounded paws/feet.
- Approximate normalized vertical allocation from the turnaround: ears/head envelope 0.52, torso 0.31, legs/feet 0.17. The ear tips end near the lower torso rather than the ground.
- Front footprint is widest at the hanging ear tips. Side depth is driven by the muzzle, rounded abdomen, and rear tail.

## Component hierarchy

- Root character group
  - body: compact pear/egg volume with a wider lower abdomen
  - head: oversized rounded volume with slightly wider cheeks and a short projecting muzzle
    - left/right ears: long tapered flattened organic volumes, attached high on the cranium and hanging laterally
    - left/right eyes: large convex dark reddish-brown surfaces with black rim/pupil and small highlights
    - nose: tiny pink rounded triangular/ovoid volume
    - mouth: restrained neutral curved line/crease
  - left/right arms: short tapered capsule volumes
    - left/right hand/paw: rounded terminal volumes, visually integrated but separately addressable
  - left/right legs: short supporting volumes
    - left/right feet: rounded, slightly forward-projecting volumes
  - tail: small round clustered/fluffy volume attached to the posterior lower torso
  - left/right weapon sockets: empty transform anchors associated with each hand

## Materials and finish

- Fur/body surfaces: warm cream/off-white dielectric, matte-to-satin, high roughness, no realistic fur strands. Subtle value separation may define muzzle/chest without texture noise.
- Inner ears and paw pads: muted warm pink dielectric, matte, slightly lower roughness than the body.
- Eyes: warm reddish-brown/amber iris, dark brown-black pupil/rim, low roughness/high specular response; small geometric highlight elements preserve readability without image textures.
- Nose: muted pink dielectric, satin.
- Mouth: dark muted brown-red line/crease.

## Identity-defining features

1. Extremely long floppy ears with tapered rounded tips and pink inset surfaces.
2. Oversized rounded head with broad soft cheeks and almost no visible neck.
3. Compact chubby pear/egg torso and very short limbs.
4. Large warm reddish-brown eyes with dark rims and strong highlight shapes.
5. Tiny centered pink nose and restrained friendly neutral mouth.
6. Rounded small feet and small fluffy spherical tail.
7. Cream/pink/brown limited palette and clean stylized surface treatment.

## Uncertainty and reconstruction policy

- The sheet supplies multiple orthographic-like views, so hidden-side uncertainty is low. Exact lens/projection and small silhouette inconsistencies between painted panels remain uncertain.
- Fur tufts are illustrative surface contours, not reliable strand geometry. They will be reduced to a few silhouette accents, keeping the web-game mesh lightweight.
- The neutral front and turnaround panels govern proportions. Pose panels do not override neutral proportions.
- The result is an approximate stylized low-poly procedural reconstruction, not a photogrammetric duplicate.
