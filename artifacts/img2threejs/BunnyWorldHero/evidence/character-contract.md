# Character reconstruction contract

- Route: standard static `character` profile; no animation/rig stage.
- Style axis: chibi/stylized animal, approximately 2.4 head units from floor to crown; the long ears extend total height to 1.0 m.
- Proportion evidence comes from the front/side/rear turnaround, not humanoid canon.
- Landmark coordinates will be normalized to the head bounding box.
- Neutral stance and bilateral reflection govern paired limbs and ears.
- Projection-first texturing is not appropriate for this character: identity is carried by clean geometry, flat cream/pink regions, glossy geometric eyes, and silhouette. The source is an illustrated multi-view sheet with baked shading rather than a photographic albedo source.
- No hair system applies. Sparse cheek/crown tufts may be modeled as silhouette geometry.
- Hidden-side confidence is high because the sheet contains front, three-quarter, side, and rear views.
- Output remains an approximate stylized procedural reconstruction; illustration-only contour variation will not be represented as fur microgeometry.
